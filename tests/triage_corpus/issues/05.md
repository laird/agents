# SSO login broken for Okta tenants — Google SSO still works

Users on Okta-backed SSO cannot log in. The Okta callback returns
successfully, but our backend rejects the resulting JWT with

```
JsonWebTokenError: invalid signature
```

We believe the Okta tenant's signing-key URL changed and our cached JWKS
is stale.

Google SSO users (about 35% of the customer base) are unaffected — they
can log in normally. Okta users (the other 65%, including all of our top
3 enterprise accounts) are blocked from the product entirely. There is no
self-serve workaround; if you don't have a Google-backed account you
can't get in.

Not a data-loss or security-bypass issue, but a major-feature outage for
the majority of paying users.
